How This Game Works

You are a government tax adviser. In each round, you will see a Hong Kong market. Your job is to set a unit tax rate to hit the government's revenue target while managing the burden on consumers and producers.

Choose wisely — the market's elasticity will determine who really pays!

Your Screen Explained

1
Scenario Card — Read this first. It describes the market and gives you clues about demand and supply elasticity.
2
S/D Diagram — Shows the demand and supply curves. After you set a tax, you'll see the supply curve shift.
3
Schedule Table — A table of prices with corresponding Qd and Qs values. Use this to check your reasoning and read off equilibrium values.
4
Tax Slider — Your main decision. Slide to set the per-unit tax rate.
5
Burden Slider — In Rounds 2–4, predict what percentage of the tax burden falls on consumers.
6
Justification Dropdowns — Explain your reasoning using the structured dropdowns. Both must be selected to submit.
7
Submit Button — Locks in your answer. You cannot change it after submitting.

How to Score High

  • Hit the revenue target as closely as possible — up to 40 points
  • In Rounds 2–4: predict the burden split accurately — up to 20 points
  • Give a good economic justification — up to 20 points (scored by your teacher)
  • Round 3 has a bonus secondary target worth up to 20 extra points

Practice Round — Widget Market

Try moving the tax slider below and watch how the supply curve shifts and the equilibrium changes. This is just for practice — no score is recorded.

Market: Widgets. Current price: $50 per widget.

Demand clue: Consumers can easily find substitutes — demand is responsive to price.

Supply clue: Factories can adjust output easily — supply is also responsive.
